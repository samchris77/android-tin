설정

Design decisions locked in

  • Accent. Teal as primary, coral as the warm CTA — not a competing accent. Teal carries calm/clinical; coral signals "act here." Avoids the previous all-orange feel.
  • Density. Comfy. Generous padding (20px screen pad, 16px card pad). TRT users carry mental load from chronic condition; clinical apps should breathe.
  • Imagery. Subtle abstract gradients + waveform shapes, no stock photos. Brain-node illustration in onboarding B; gradient washes on dark hero cards. Keeps the app feeling clinical, not lifestyle-marketing.
  • Onboarding card style. Hero illustration above a tight copy block; one paragraph (3 sentences max) per page. Pill badge → eyebrow + bold heading; weak dots → thin coral progress bar OR fattened active dot.
  • Matcher dial. Hairline circular dial (Direction A) is the conservative pick — keeps muscle memory from the existing screen. Log slider (B) and audiogram fader (C) are the bolder bets — try B if vertical space is the priority.
  • Volume + play. Always inside one rounded "dock" — visually a single control surface, not two unrelated rows. Removes the previous disconnect.
  • Stepper buttons. Stripped to ±5 / ±50 only, mono-digit, hairline-bordered. Replaces the chunky cream embossed +/−100 row. Less childish.
  • Bottom nav. 4 tabs (홈 / 소리 / 기록 / 설정) per the markdown doc. The design-system 5-tab exploration ("Learn") is not adopted here — learning materials live inside 설정 → 학습 자료. Revisit if education modules grow past 3–4.
  • Korean copy register. -하기 verbs, generic "설문조사" not "TFI" in user-facing strings, no exclamation marks, no gamified celebration language.

Open questions surfaced by these mocks

  • Which 홈 wins? A (player-first) is the strongest aesthetically and matches the design system's hero-player exploration. B (ring-first) is the safest clinical pick. C (equipment panel) is the most ownable but risks "cure device" framing. D (timeline) is the best argument for TRT's long arc. I'd ship A as the default but use D's week strip as a secondary widget.
  • Mixing-point UI. Direction A on 홈 introduces a coral "▲ Mixing point" tick on the volume slider. This implies MixingPointCalibration data exists — currently Tier 1 in plan.md. Either gate this UI behind calibration, or show the tick as a heuristic estimate with a "calibrate" CTA.
  • Onboarding length. All three directions assume 7 pages. The storybook variant (B) makes 7 feel long; the module variant (C) makes 7 feel right. Decide the count after the explainer copy is locked.
  • Matcher subscreen vs. tab. Open question from navigation.md still unresolved. These mocks assume subscreen.
  • Heatmap (Records B). Beautiful but only meaningful after ~6 weeks of data. Need a first-month state for the screen that doesn't look empty.
