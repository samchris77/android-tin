# Product Requirements Document

## Vision
A clinically-grounded Android companion for people with chronic subjective tinnitus, designed around the TRT principle that the goal is **habituation of reaction**, not silence.

## Problem
Most consumer tinnitus apps make the same three mistakes:
1. They **mask** instead of habituate.
2. They **measure loudness** instead of distress.
3. They **optimize for short engagement** when the actual treatment timeline is 12–24 months.

Korean-speaking users have limited access to TRT-aligned digital tools in their language.

## Target user
- Adults with chronic subjective tinnitus (≥6 months duration)
- Korean-speaking, primary market Korea
- Motivated to engage with a 12–24 month habituation arc
- Either independently managing, or working alongside an ENT/audiologist

## Audience for this PRD
- Project owner
- Clinical reviewers evaluating clinical alignment

## What it IS
- A daily companion for the long TRT / sound-therapy arc
- A **sub-mixing-point** sound therapy delivery tool
- A distress-tracking journal anchored on validated instruments (TFI)
- A red-flag screener that routes users to medical care when warranted
- A clinically-grounded education layer based on TRT directive counseling

## What it ISN'T
- **Not a clinician replacement.** It complements care; it does not substitute for ENT/audiologist evaluation or treatment planning.
- **Not a masker.** Masking prevents habituation. Sound therapy is delivered below the mixing point. This is a core differentiator.
- **Not a community / social platform.** No forums, peer chat, sharing, or comparative metrics.
- **Not a hearing-aid replacement.** Hearing loss is detected and referred, not treated in-app.
- **Not for pulsatile or red-flag tinnitus.** Pulsatile tinnitus, sudden unilateral hearing loss, vertigo, neurological signs, or severe new headache trigger a hard referral. These are never managed in-app.

## Platform & market (current scope)
- **Platform:** Android only (iOS not in scope)
- **Market:** Korea only
- **Language:** Korean (primary)
- **Pricing:** Free
