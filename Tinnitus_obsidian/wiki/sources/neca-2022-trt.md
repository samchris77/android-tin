# NECA HTA: 이명재훈련치료 (TRT) — 2022

**Citation:** 한국보건의료연구원 (NECA). 의료기술재평가보고서 2022: 이명재훈련치료. 보고서번호 NECA-R-21-001-12. 김유림(담당연구원), 박지정(부담당연구원). 2022년 1월.

- **Type:** Health Technology Assessment (HTA) — systematic review + GRADE evidence appraisal + national reimbursement recommendation
- **Year:** 2022 (assessment date: 2022-01-14)
- **Population / N:** Patients with tinnitus refractory to pharmacotherapy. Evidence base = 11 studies (systematic review).
- **Intervention:** TRT (이명재훈련치료) = directive counseling (지시적 상담) + sound therapy (소리치료) per Jastreboff protocol. Variants accepted as TRT: habituational therapy, CBT + sound therapy, modified TRT, TRT + hearing aid.
- **Comparators:** Counseling (상담), cognitive therapy (인지치료 — includes tinnitus coping training, CBT, ACT), tinnitus total masking (이명차폐), no treatment (무치료).
- **Primary outcomes:** Tinnitus disability scores (THI, TFI, TQ, TQI), tinnitus improvement rate, quality of life.
- **Result:** Conditionally Recommended (조건부 권고함) for refractory tinnitus, 2022-01-14 의료기술재평가위원회.
- **Risk of bias / quality:** Cochrane RoB applied to each included study; assessments by 2 independent reviewers.
- **Updates this caused in the wiki:** Updated [[wiki/clinical/trt]], [[wiki/clinical/sound-therapy]], [[wiki/clinical/cbt]], [[wiki/clinical/habituation]]; informs [[wiki/app/prd]] (Korean regulatory context).

## Key findings

- **Safety: HIGH (GRADE).** Only adverse event reported: 1 study reporting ear canal swelling/pain from sound generator — considered minor and comparable to hearing-aid/earphone use.
- **Efficacy vs counseling or CBT (combined comparator):** No statistically significant difference.
  - Tinnitus disability score (9 studies): SMD −0.11, 95% CI −0.41 to 0.20, p=0.49, I²=74%.
  - Improvement rate (5 studies): RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.71–1.71, p=0.66, I²=53%.
- **Efficacy vs counseling alone (8 studies):** Disability score not significantly different (SMD −0.25, 95% CI −0.61 to 0.11, p=0.17, I²=75%). **However**, improvement rate (3 studies) significantly higher for TRT: RR 1.53, 95% CI 1.04–2.26, p=0.03, I²=0%.
- **Efficacy vs cognitive therapy (3 studies):** Not significantly different (SMD −0.24, p=0.33, I²=65%).
- **Efficacy vs tinnitus masking (3 studies):** TRT significantly better. SMD −0.74, 95% CI −1.16 to −0.32, p=0.0006, I²=0%. Improvement rate not different (RR 1.10, p=0.69).
- **Efficacy vs no treatment (2 studies):** TRT significantly better (p=0.013 and p<0.001).
- **GRADE for efficacy:** Very Low to Moderate, depending on outcome and comparator.
- **Committee verdict:** "이명재훈련치료를 이명의 다양한 치료방법 중 하나로 사용할 수 있는 효과적인 치료" — effective and safe; one of several usable treatments. Decision: **조건부 권고함 (Conditionally Recommended)**.

### Korean regulatory and market context

- TRT classification: 보건복지부 고시 제2002-68호 (2002-09-27), registered before the new medical-technology assessment regime.
- Insurance status: **비급여** (non-reimbursed). Classification 소-4, EDI code MZ004.
- Required procedure components (per 건강보험심사평가원 고시): (1) audiological/internal exam result explanation, (2) tinnitus mechanism explanation with figures/models, (3) TRT process explanation, (4) Q&A about patient's tinnitus, (5) sound-therapy necessity + how-to (with sound generator), (6) survey/cognition assessment. Minimum session duration: 30–40 minutes.
- Quoted historical claim from the gazette: symptom improvement rate ≥80%, tinnitus blocking 20%. (Note: this is from the original 2002 listing, not validated by the 2022 HTA.)
- **Korean-market devices** for TRT sound generation (식약처 registered, 2021):
  - Starkey TM-R (스타키코리아)
  - AMZ17ITC-DW-LP (지엔히어링코리아)
  - Wave 2G (우리와우보청기)
  - J-TRT (제일보청기)
  - Ti-Masker (지엔히어링코리아)
- Subcommittee noted trend of hearing aids partially substituting for dedicated sound generators as hearing-aid performance improves.
- International coding: no specific TRT CPT code in the US — billed under physical medicine (CPT 97014), speech therapy (92507), or psychotherapy (90832–90838). No Japanese reimbursement code identified.

### Theoretical model used

Report endorses Jastreboff's Neurophysiological Model (그림 1.1): tinnitus signal → 청각여과기/중추신경계 → 인식과 평가 (auditory cortex) + 정서의 연계성 (limbic) → 신체반응 (autonomic). See [[wiki/clinical/habituation]] for the wiki page on this model.

## Quality concerns

None — this is a government HTA performed by a credentialed national agency with explicit Cochrane RoB and GRADE methodology. The only caveat: the included evidence base for efficacy is itself heterogeneous (I² often >50%) and many trials are small, which is why GRADE for efficacy ranged Very Low to Moderate rather than High.

## Our take

This is **the canonical Korean evidence document** for TRT and should be cited whenever the wiki or the app makes a claim about TRT efficacy, safety, or regulatory status in Korea. Three key implications for the app:

1. **Don't oversell TRT efficacy.** vs counseling or CBT, TRT shows no statistical advantage on disability scores. Its strongest signal is vs tinnitus masking and vs no treatment. The app's TRT-framed content should position TRT as one effective option, not the option.
2. **CBT is a defensible primary modality.** Given equivalence with TRT in this HTA — and CBT's better evidence base in [[wiki/sources/walter-2025]] and [[wiki/sources/bauer-2018]] — the app's CBT-forward strategy is well-supported. See [[wiki/app/cbt-program]].
3. **Korean reimbursement reality**: TRT is 비급여 in Korea. Users will pay out of pocket for clinic-delivered TRT. App-based scaffolding has clear social-value justification.
