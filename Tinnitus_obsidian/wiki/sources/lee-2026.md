## Key findings

- Korean tinnitus prevalence (KNHANES 2009–2012): 20.7%; mild 69.2%, moderate 27.9%, severe 3.0%.
- Frames a paradigm shift from peripheral auditory pathology to CNS network disease: maladaptive neuroplasticity + thalamocortical dysrhythmia as the dominant modern model.
- Auditory deafferentation cascade: ↑spontaneous firing → ↓lateral inhibition → ↑central gain → Δ/θ–γ coupling → thalamocortical dysrhythmia → tonotopic reorganization.
- Triple Network Model (SN/CEN/DMN) explains the transition from acute to chronic tinnitus and why attention/emotional systems sustain the percept. Cite: De Ridder et al., Clin Exp Otorhinolaryngol 2022.
- TRT mechanism reviewed: directive counseling + sound therapy induces vmPFC to classify tinnitus as low-salience noise → reinforces thalamic reticular nucleus → gates tinnitus signal → habituation over 6–18 months.
- rTMS meta-analysis (He Z et al., Front Neurosci 2025, n=not specified): short-term THI −13 pts, VAS −1.85; **6-month effect not statistically significant** (−4.2, 95% CI −10.28 to +1.76). TPJ target outperforms auditory cortex alone (meta-analysis: 100% vs 50% responder rate, Watson et al., Otolaryngol HNS 2023).
- tDCS combination: Cathodal F3 + anodal F4 (bifrontal DLPFC) + tRNS left temporal cortex showed greatest improvement in tinnitus severity and QoL (Chen JJ et al., JAMA Otolaryngol HNS 2020).
- **Critical finding:** Neuromodulation alone is ineffective. Must be combined with TRT or CBT — neuromodulation cannot retag tinnitus as low-salience without concurrent habituation therapy.
- Auricular VNS (cymba conchae) paired with tailored notched sound is being used clinically; device 알파-스팀 sold commercially in Korea (FDA approval for MDD 2005, repurposed). Individual variation in ABVN anatomy limits consistency.

## Quality concerns

This is a lecture slide deck, not a peer-reviewed publication. Several claims about neuromodulation effectiveness are drawn from meta-analyses with small included study counts and heterogeneous protocols. The long-term (6-month) rTMS data specifically does **not** reach statistical significance despite being presented alongside positive short-term figures — this distinction must be preserved in all downstream wiki pages.

알파-스팀 is a commercially available device cited here as a VNS option. Its evidence base for tinnitus specifically (vs. MDD) is not detailed in this lecture. Apply watchlist scrutiny in [[wiki/clinical/vns-auricular]].

## My / our take

Excellent clinical orientation lecture covering the conceptual arc from peripheral → CNS model, through TRT mechanics, to neuromodulation. Most useful for: (1) building the pathophysiology and network model concept pages, (2) populating the rTMS and tDCS therapy pages with realistic clinical protocols and honest effect-size caveats. The "neuromodulation alone doesn't work" emphasis is a critical guardrail for the app — any feature touching brain stimulation must contextualize it within TRT/CBT, not as a standalone cure.
